How points work
  • Tap a block when it's done to bank its points. Tapped one by mistake? Tap it again to undo.
  • Big blocks pay more: photo editing 30, workouts, pottery, laundromat and meal prep 25, smaller tasks 5–20.
  • Skin care has its own tab: morning face 10, night face 10 (the night shows which treatment), body + hands 5.
  • Clear every block in a day, schedule and skin care, for a bonus: +50 on weekdays, +25 on weekends.
  • Nothing resets at midnight. Every day is saved, and you can tap any past day in the grid to check off a block you forgot.
  • The meter tracks a 30-day run. A perfect 30 days is about 4,900 points, so 4,200 fills with a solid month, even with a few off days. Then a new 30-day run starts.
  • Points are saved in this browser, so use the app on the same phone each day.
